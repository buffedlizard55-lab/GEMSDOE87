## Mandatory starting brief — read before every session

This section preserves the operational substance of the project prompt. It is intentionally at the top of the repository so future work begins with the constraints rather than a fresh, unrecorded guess.

1. **Goal:** produce scientifically grounded, distinct, portal-valid GeoTIFF predictions for the DOE GEMS competition; improve the probability of a real score without inventing one.
2. **Unique-lane rule:** before dot placement, compare the continuous surface against the public registry. After placement, compare final dots. Stop and log a duplicate if any registry raster has rank correlation `>= 0.90` or if **more than** `70%` of candidate dots lie within `3` pixels of one registry raster’s dots.
3. **Holdout rule:** withhold whole fault segments with a spatial buffer; derive any catalogue-based feature only from visible faults; mask visible faults pixel-exactly; score local DTI with triangular `300 m` kernel, `alpha=0.2`, `beta=0.8`.
4. **Evidence labels:** a model measurement is **HOLDOUT-DTI** (include evaluator version, withheld-positive count, and 95% CI). A competition score is **ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED** only when copied from an organizer receipt. A projection is never a score.
5. **Leakage rule:** test every feature alone on spatial holdout. Oriented AUC above `0.90` is leakage until proven otherwise.
6. **Submission gate:** do not use a weekly slot or publish a downloadable candidate until it beats the current matched holdout control, passes the leakage canary, passes both uniqueness gates, and passes the written-byte GeoTIFF validator.
7. **Budget:** no more than three experiments or two hours per research round. Negative results are deliverables. Promotion to a real weekly slot is a separate decision.
8. **No manual raster inspection:** the project must generate the file, re-open it, validate it, produce a SHA-256, and state plainly whether it is safe to download. Browser upload is necessarily an account-holder action; no human geospatial inspection is required by this project.
9. **Research discipline:** propose/rank 3–5 hypotheses before implementation; name layers, physical signature, off-catalogue mechanism, non-fault mimic, novelty, expected local value, and external-data status. Never call external data “officially verified” unless directly obtained and checked from its official source.
10. **Core Values:** **Maximize P(Win)** means choose the evidence-preserving path that maximizes the chance of a genuine result, not the most exciting story. **Own the Outcome** means ship the validation, download, source status, and limitations with the model—not a partial result that someone else must reconstruct.
11. **Shared engineering:** use the common template, metric, spatial-holdout, duplicate-registry, validator, and release infrastructure where it is sound; repair shared logic instead of preserving a private workaround. A correction must be versioned, rerun, documented in the irregularity register, and propagated to the card/site.
12. **Data discipline:** obtain any new scientific layer from a free public official source, check availability/license/provenance, hash it, and test it independently before training. A blocked official source stays blocked; no owner mirror is silently relabeled as official.
13. **Lane discipline:** operate in one declared method lane at a time. Registry artifacts may only support anti-duplication gates, never training, hyperparameter selection, or point placement. Do not tune a promoted lane against its already-used folds.
14. **Delivery discipline:** maintain the downloadable TIFF, exact SHA-256, machine-readable card, executive upload instructions, source/review links, change/irregularity record, and a Pages-ready static site. Uploading through a competition account and any organizer result remain outside this local project unless a real receipt is captured.

The full structured implementation of this brief is in the [single active-lane declaration](registry/lane.md), [`registry/hypotheses.json`](registry/hypotheses.json), [`registry/source_ledger.json`](registry/source_ledger.json), and the run card.

### 3. H87-FRB method

**Hypothesis:** a previously unmapped, buried, or oblique transfer structure can rotate local fabric across a narrow corridor without producing a single obvious surface scarp.

**Label-free physical stack:**

- detrended elevation (band 12), total magnetic intensity (band 14), and isostatic gravity anomaly (band 13) supply independent local axial orientations;
- their multi-scale double-angle **orientation rotation/discordance** forms the fabric-boundary signal;
- geodetic shear and dilation (bands 7–8) provide strain-energy and strain-boundary corroboration;
- conductivity (band 17) supplies a subsurface edge corroborator;
- no distance-to-catalogue channel is built or used.

**Named non-fault mimic:** lithologic contacts, volcanic-flow margins, drainage divides, and processing seams can rotate texture and yield the same signal. This is why the candidate is not called a fault map and why the holdout is only a proxy.

**Important negative sub-result:** the fixed, label-free H87-FRB surface scored lower than the calibrated model on the visible-catalogue proxy. The released field is a supervised calibration of the geological stack; it is **not** proof that the raw physical surface alone identifies expert-hidden faults.

## Forensic answer: why the named GEMSDOE32 H33 artifact may look strong

The prompt attributes `h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros` a value of 0.2778. **That score-to-file attribution is not verified here.** The current owner-maintained [`submission_build.json`](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE32/blob/780a5a534269c79adfe6d1d7d5aadead161332aa/registry/submission_build.json) instead says that no organizer score exists for the artifact. Its [byte audit](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE32/blob/780a5a534269c79adfe6d1d7d5aadead161332aa/docs/downloads/gemsdoe32-h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-audit.json) reports a 37,654-dot raster and a catalogue-flank removal rule, but it is an **OWNER-REPORT**, not an organizer receipt.

At a doctoral-methods level, the plausible *mechanism* is clear while the claimed score is not:

1. The triangular 300-m distance kernel gives no additional truth credit when dots redundantly cover the same nearby target, while predicted mass can still contribute false-positive cost.
2. Sparse, approximately kernel-separated emission can therefore improve the coverage-to-mass ratio relative to dense ribbons or blobs.
3. Removing visible-catalogue-proximal dots can preserve emission budget for locations not intentionally masked by the local evaluation protocol.
4. None of these deductions identifies hidden expert-labelled faults or proves a portal result. A sparse raster can score better for metric reasons and still be geologically wrong.

**Can this repository promise a score above 0.2778, 0.3195, or 0.4413? No.** Those values are treated as **USER-REPORT** unless an official dated receipt is supplied. The public leaderboard is dynamic, hidden truth is unavailable, and a visible-catalogue holdout is not numerically comparable to its score. H87-FRB establishes a local proxy improvement and a valid unique file—not a predicted leaderboard ranking.

## Limitations and next work

1. **Need an organizer receipt/hash crosswalk.** It is the only way to determine whether the named H33 artifact actually earned 0.2778 and to label future results ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED.
2. **Need a genuinely independent off-catalogue validation population.** Visible-catalogue recovery may not transfer to hidden expert-new faults. The current release should not consume scarce slots merely because it passes this proxy.
3. **Need direct official external-data provenance before H87-RHA or H87-SCA.** Obtain GeoDAWN radiometric grids from the USGS release and spring/chemistry data from GDR 1391 on an unrestricted, auditable environment; hash, license, and feature-audit them before use.
4. **Do not tune H87-FRB further against these four folds.** If another experiment is justified, use the pre-ranked H87-SSB with a new preregistration and retain the H87-FRB result unchanged.
5. **Re-run `scripts/sync_registry.py` immediately before any revised release.** The current maximum 3-pixel overlap is below the written stop rule but not far from it.
