## IR-87-V2-09 — Root cause of `Band 18` gravity crest failure: `iso_grav_anom_hg` is signed $\partial g/\partial x$, whereas `Band 05` is isotropic $|\nabla_H g|$

- **Observed:** Auditing `training_features.tif` directly against finite-difference derivatives of `Band 13` (`iso_grav_anom`) proves that **`Band 18` (`iso_grav_anom_hg`) is signed directional $\partial B_{13}/\partial x$** (`49.1%` negative inside the footprint, `r = 0.0078` with isotropic $|\nabla_H B_{13}|$), whereas **`Band 05` (`iso_grav_anom_slope`) is the true isotropic horizontal gradient magnitude $|\nabla_H B_{13}|$** (`r = +0.9619`, strictly non-negative). - **Decision:** Use `Band 05` (`iso_grav_anom_slope`) + isotropic `|\nabla_H(\text{RTP})|` (`Band 02`) exclusively for subsurface fault-step crest scoring in `AXIS = RELIEF`, and propose `ISOTROPIC_GRAV_SNAP` for Weeks 3–4 in `SLOT_PLAN.md`.
