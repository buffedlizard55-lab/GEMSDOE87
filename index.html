What the challenge evaluates

Fault traces, not geothermal prospectivity alone.

The official task asks for fault predictions across the GeoDAWN study area. Faults can provide pathways for geothermal fluids, but a geophysical anomaly or thermal signal is not, by itself, proof of a fault.
