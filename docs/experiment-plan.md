## 1. Why `GEMSDOE32` (`0.2778`) Succeeded & Why Subsequent Lanes Plateaued

Across the full `buffedlizard55-lab` fleet (`GEMSDOE1`–`GEMSDOE88`), two empirical discoveries explain the exact gap between the live `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED` leaderboard ceiling (`0.2778` on `GEMSDOE32/h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros.tif` [3], `0.2478` on `GEMSDOE70/p8` [4], `0.2442` on `19GEMSDOE` [5]) and recent internal lanes:

1. **Down-Dip Hanging-Wall Geometric Defocusing on Dipping Normal Faults (New Physical Discovery):**
   Great Basin range-front and intrabasin normal faults dip $\theta \approx 50^\circ\text{–}65^\circ$ beneath alluvial hanging-wall basins (`depth_to_base_surf` up to `4.5 km`). Measuring `iso_grav_anom_hg` across `25,000` normal-fault cross sections on `existing_faults.tif` along the label-free regional dip vector $\hat{\mathbf{n}}(\mathbf{x}) = (n_y, n_x)$ reveals that the peak horizontal gravity gradient is **not centered on the surface fault trace (`s = 0 m`)**:
   - Footwall (`s = -1000 m`): `-0.1534` mean standardized `iso_grav_anom_hg`
   - Surface fault trace (`s = 0 m`): `-0.0589`
   - Hanging wall (`s = +600 m`): `+0.0715`
   - Hanging wall (`s = +1200 m`): `+0.2051` (`+600 m` to `+1,400 m` down-dip offset — completely outside the `300 m` DTI evaluation halo!).
   The exact same down-dip displacement affects hanging-wall smectite clay-cap / brine MT conductivity (`mt_res_1000m`, `mt_res_2000m`), aeromagnetic RTP edges (`mag_tc`, `tmi_up150`), and down-gradient thermal outflow plumes (`temp_c`, `F2mDAB`), while footwall fluvial steepness (`ksn_max_m0.9`) terminates on the up-dip side (`s = -200` to `-500 m`). Isotropic pixel-wise GBDTs blur these oppositely offset signatures instead of migrating them back to the surface trace.

2. **Two-Regime Evaluation Geometry & SGMC Input Contamination:**
   - Purely catalogue-conditioned relay lanes (`GEMSDOE78` `H78-A`/`GSC`) concentrate `>70%` of predicted dots near mapped fault tips (`d_vis <= 10 px`), driving `HOLDOUT-DTI` high on held-out Quaternary segments (`0.1898`) while starving unmapped piedmonts (`Instrument C_pied = 0.0915`) and breaching the `0.70` 3-px overlap gate against wide-halo prior rasters (`57GEMSDOE`, `GEMSDOE3`).
   - Conversely, `GEMSDOE32` (`H33-2-B2`, live `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED` `0.2778`) used **zero catalogue distance inputs** and spaced dots (`d_min = 3 px`, `37,654` dots), placing `39.1%` of dots in greenfield basins/piedmonts (`d_cat > 25 px`) and scoring `C_pied = 0.09164` (`B' = 0.09463`).
   - Audit finding: `GEMSDOE80` (`MACS-61`) included `sub_sgmc_bedrock_prox` (`USGS SGMC` fault distance) inside its training matrix `X` (`src/gems80/macs61_features.py` lines `466-468`), which both contaminates any `SGMC`-derived evaluation (`C_pied`, `B'`) and injects pre-Cenozoic Mesozoic thrust/pluton contact false positives. **Lane G87 strictly forbids any `SGMC` input feature in `X` (`0` SGMC columns everywhere).**
