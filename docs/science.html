$$\text{DTI}(S, G) = \frac{\text{TP}_w(S, G)}{0.2\,\text{TP}_w(S, G) + 0.2\,\text{FP}_w(S, G) + 0.8\,|G|}$$

Because deleting 3,891 dots inside $0 < d_{\text{cat}} \le 1.0\text{ px}$ (100 m digitization halo around already-published existing_faults.tif) removes pure false positives ($\Delta \text{TP}w \approx 0$, $\Delta \text{FP}w = -3{,}891$), inverting $\text{DTI}(D{2.8}) = 0.2600$ and $\text{DTI}(H{27\text{-}4}) = 0.2708$ yields:

$$\text{TP}w(D{2.8}, G) \approx 5{,}074.7\text{ weighted px}, \qquad |G| \approx 13{,}420\text{ hidden fault px across Nevada}$$

Substituting $\Delta \text{FP}w = -6{,}436$ for H33-2-B2 ($d{\text{cat}} > 2.0\text{ px}$) predicts $\widehat{\text{DTI}}(H_{33\text{-}2\text{-}B2}) = \mathbf{0.2784}$ versus user-reported 0.2778 (0.0006 discrepancy).
