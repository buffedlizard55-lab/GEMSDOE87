Why such thinning could help

Under the stated triangular-kernel Tversky formulation, clustered dots compete for the same nearby truth credit while still carrying false-positive mass. A sparse placement at the kernel scale can improve the coverage-to-mass trade-off. Masking known visible-fault pixels avoids placing mass where the local protocol deliberately excludes them.

This is metric reasoning, not proof that the artifact earned a particular portal value.
