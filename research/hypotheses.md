## Physical evidence and limits

- [DrivenData problem/metric/submission specification](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/): the target is **fault presence**, not geothermal vent coordinates. The hidden labels include expert-identified faults absent from the public catalogue. A fault prediction does not establish permeability, temperature, or an economic geothermal resource.
- [USGS GeoDAWN release](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and), DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ: airborne magnetic data support subsurface structural mapping. East-west production flight lines, north-south tie lines, block boundaries, and variable terrain clearance are documented artifact risks.
- [USGS: Matching Magnetic Trends and Patterns Across the Tintina Fault](https://pubs.usgs.gov/sir/2007/5289/SIR2007-5289-C.pdf): magnetic pattern correspondence can constrain displacement. This is a different region and scale, **not validation** of a sub-kilometre GeoDAWN detector.
- [USGS: Aeromagnetic expression of faults that offset basin fill](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/aeromagnetic-expression-faults-offset-basin-fill-albuquerque-basin-new-mexico): concealed basin-fill faults can have magnetic expressions. This supports the possibility, not the precision, of this hypothesis.
- [INGENIOUS official project](https://gbcge.org/current-projects/ingenious/) and [GDR dataset 1391](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391): contextual geothermal datasets and licensing; neither is used as new training data in this lane.
